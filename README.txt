# 0–10 V to 4–20 mA Converter

A simple analog voltage-to-current converter designed with KiCad.

## Project Objective

Design a PCB that converts a 0–10 V input signal into a proportional 4–20 mA output.

## Target

| Vin | Iout |
|---:|---:|
| 0 V | 4 mA |
| 2.5 V | 8 mA |
| 5 V | 12 mA |
| 7.5 V | 16 mA |
| 10 V | 20 mA |

## Tools

- KiCad
- LTspice
